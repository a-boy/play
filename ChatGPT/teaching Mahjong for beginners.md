teaching Mahjong for beginners
----

Teaching Mahjong to beginners can be a lot of fun! The game can seem complicated at first, but once you break it down, it becomes easier to understand. Here's how you can teach it step by step:

### 1. **Introduce the Basic Rules**

Start with the general goal of the game:

* **Objective**: The goal of Mahjong is to form a complete set of 14 tiles (known as a "hand") using combinations of **pairs**, **pungs**, **chows**, and **kongs**.

  * **Pair**: A pair consists of two identical tiles.
  * **Pung**: A pung is three identical tiles.
  * **Chow**: A chow is a straight sequence of three consecutive numbers in the same suit (like 2-3-4 of bamboo).
  * **Kong**: A kong is four identical tiles. (More on this later.)

### 2. **Tile Types Overview**

Explain the different kinds of tiles:

* **Suits**: There are three suits: **Bamboo**, **Characters**, and **Circles**. Each suit has numbers from 1 to 9.
* **Honor Tiles**: These include **Winds** (East, South, West, North) and **Dragons** (Red, Green, White).
* **Bonus Tiles**: These are **Flowers** and **Seasons**. They aren’t essential to the basic gameplay, but they give extra points when drawn.

### 3. **Setup**

* **Tile Distribution**: In a standard game, each player starts with 13 tiles, and they draw one more tile (to have 14). The tiles are shuffled and stacked in the center of the table in a square.

### 4. **Gameplay Flow**

* **Turns**: Players take turns to **draw** a tile from the wall and **discard** a tile. They aim to build their hand by forming sets (pung, chow, pair) from their tiles.
* **Calling Tiles**: Players can “call” tiles that others discard to complete their hand:

  * **Pung**: You can claim a discarded tile to complete a pung (three identical tiles).
  * **Chow**: You can claim a discarded tile if it completes a chow (a sequence of three consecutive tiles in the same suit).
  * **Kong**: If you have three identical tiles and someone discards the same tile, you can declare a kong (four identical tiles). A kong gives an additional tile.

### 5. **Winning**

* **Mahjong**: A player wins when they complete a valid hand, which is 14 tiles consisting of 4 sets (pung, chow, or kong) and 1 pair. Once a player wins, the round ends.

### 6. **Basic Strategy Tips**

* **Focus on the pairs and your hand progression early on**: Don’t worry too much about making a perfect hand right away. Focus on completing sets.
* **Watch other players' discards**: This will help you understand what they’re working toward.
* **Don't be too greedy**: Sometimes it’s better to let a tile go rather than trying to force a hand.

### 7. **Additional Rules to Introduce Later**

* **Kong Bonus**: After declaring a kong, the player draws an extra tile from the wall. If they declare a kong from someone else’s discard, the player who made the discard has to draw an extra tile from the wall as well.
* **Flower Tiles**: These tiles can be collected for extra points but do not count toward making a hand.
